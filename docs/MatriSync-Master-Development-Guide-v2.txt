3. Product Definition and Names
MatriSync is an AI-assisted, offline-first maternal and child healthcare continuity system. It aims to reduce loss or fragmentation of critical information and referral responsibility when care moves between healthcare professionals/facilities, including in low-connectivity settings. It also supports longer-term maternal/child records, clinician-scheduled follow-ups, and mother-facing education.
Core problem: A physical referral slip may travel with a patient while advance communication, completeness, arrival acknowledgement, prior treatment context and feedback to the sending facility remain uncertain. Paper documents still matter and must not be discarded. MatriSync complements them.
Retained name: MatriSathi — mother/caregiver-only educational and supportive area. The conversational subfeature is called AI Companion until specifically named later.
Do not use MatriFlow, MatriWatch, MatriBridge, MatriVitals, MatriBand, or other proprietary sub-brand names anywhere in the visible UI, unless preserving an old source-code identifier temporarily is required to prevent breakage; if so, the visible label must use the v2 term and remaining internal renaming is documented.
Provisional tagline: Every Mother. Every Step. — candidate only; trademark clearance and final approval not established. Store in one configurable brand constant. Do not state that it is registered or finalized.
Safety boundary: AI and software assist with documentation, validated reference lookup, approved follow-up instructions and administrative workflow. Neither AI nor app autonomously diagnoses, selects treatment/hospital, prescribes medication, adjusts doses, generates clinical risk scores, or makes clinical decisions. Patient-specific interaction alerts need documented approval, validation and applicable regulatory review before activation; renaming them “educational” does not negate their clinical-decision-support character.
4. Roles, Identity and Access
Three public role families only:
1. Mother / Caregiver — own authorized health information, linked child profiles, follow-ups and MatriSathi; never facility inventory controls.
2. Healthcare Professional — verified future clinician/worker functions; medical records, clinician-authorized prescriptions, professional approval of referral package. Exact doctor/nurse/ANM/ASHA privileges must be implemented through future approved verified credentials, not role-picker trust.
3. Healthcare Organization — verified facility staff/operations: referral initiation and receiving workflow, facility patients (authorized view), staff management, NFC stock, device inventory, refunds and transactions; no automatic clinical editing rights.
Distribution Management is a restricted organizational capability, not a fourth public role choice. It can be previewed from Organization > More for the synthetic demo; production access requires verified distributor entitlement and cannot be granted from a public role selector.
Auth account UID ≠ clinical Patient ID ≠ Referral ID ≠ engraved NFC band Inventory ID. Child IDs must be distinct from mother IDs; patient-entered information is distinguished from professionally verified records.
Phase 1: role choice selects only a synthetic demonstration workspace. Firebase account creation authenticates identity, not a clinician license, hospital affiliation, or access to patient records.

6. Exact Clinical Referral & Information Workflows (Future, Not Live Yet)
6.1 Doctor-side clinical preparation
1. Verified doctor assesses patient, captures reason, relevant medical/pregnancy history, prescriptions/treatments, vital observations and supporting reports.
2. AI drafts structured referral and checks missing/unclear/inconsistent documentation. It cites original reports with time, units, source and author, and flags unknowns. It does not decide treatment or destination.
3. Doctor reviews and approves; preserve audit/version history. Clinical decisions are never silently replaced by an AI draft. Urgent transfer remains possible when fields are unavailable.
4. Approved package enters organization's Ready to Initiate queue.
6.2 Connected-facility operational referral
1. Sending organization selects approved package, picks permitted destination, and initiates digital transfer.
2. Receiving facility accepts/declines with a reason where authorized. Pre-arrival awareness requires connectivity or another communication path.
3. Patient arrives; authorized staff verify identity and record arrival.
4. Clinical handoff reviewed and acknowledged as a separate event; professional controls clinical decisions.
5. Referral closure/outcome documented; sending facility and authorized patient profile show synced status. Pending/in-transit statuses are not proofs of patient location. Distinguish server-synced events from local pending events.
6.3 External/non-participating destination referral
1. After clinical approval, organization prepares comprehensive printed hard copy, complete organized digital soft copy, and essential NFC handoff.
2. Paper goes to patient/caregiver; authorized digital copy sent only if family has a phone and a suitable secure route. Patient smartphone not required.
3. Facility uses approved BLE ESP32 read/write device to write essential handoff and verify read-back. NFC band does not store the full report, PDF or historical record.
4. Patient transports documentation. A hospital without MatriSync and without reader equipment can still use paper/soft copy.
5. No fabricated acceptance/arrival statuses. Document verified follow-up by phone/other channels when legitimately available.
6.4 Repeat investigations and trust
- Store original issuing laboratory/hospital, collection/report dates, units, source attachments, professional verification and updated versions.
- Show previous results to the receiving professional. Never guarantee a receiving facility will accept prior reports or prevent medically necessary repeat tests. Repeat decisions remain clinical.
6.5 Handoff readiness and responsive updates
- Handoff document completeness checklist and clinician approvals; sensitive fields may be unavailable with explicit acknowledgement. Never stop emergency care solely for form completion.
- If a document changes, mark the previously prepared summary/version as potentially outdated and require review and re-approval.
- Offline handoff and remote network acknowledgement are separate, with clear local and server status.
7. NFC Device, Tags and Inventory Architecture (Future)
Facility-owned Bluetooth NFC Reader/Writer: proposed ESP32 MCU with BLE, compatible NFC read/write module, small screen, physical controls and portable power. The app/web communicates by BLE where available; browser Web Bluetooth is platform-limited; native adapter needed for broader supported devices. ESP32 receives compact encrypted payload, shows size/ready, writes tag after physical confirmation, reads back to verify and returns success/error to app. It also reads authorized tags and transfers encrypted payload to app for review/decryption. Do not include direct NFC phone APIs in the app.
Patient-carried NFC band: passive, no battery, issued by facility. Only compact essential encrypted clinical handoff resides in the writable clinical NFC payload, sized to tag capacity. It may contain minimal necessary patient identification, patient clinical ID, referral/handoff ID, approved urgent history/warnings and metadata. Not entire history or PDFs. The asset serial is printed/engraved on the outside only and is not put into the clinical payload. Permanent asset ID is never a permanent patient ID.
Separate identities:
- patient_id: stable clinical person identity.
- referral_id: a particular care-transition/handoff.
- band_asset_code: durable printed/engraved physical identifier for stock; no clinical meaning.
Band lifecycle: manufacture/receive → warehouse/facility stock → issue and optional deposit → clinical use → return at participating facility → condition check and refund where applicable → distributor collection and facility reimbursement → secure erase/reinitialize + hygiene verification → reuse OR replace/retire. Stock may move between surplus and shortage facilities through authorized transfers and central distributor oversight; transaction ordering/reconciliation must survive offline queued events.
Safety before clinical rollout: per-facility offline decryption/authorized keys, data minimization, tag capacity and write endurance, cryptographic integrity and freshness, lost band/device response, reuse clearance, infection control, device authentication and BLE pairing, patient matching, audit, refunds and fraud safeguards. These are not implemented in Phase 1.
8. MatriSathi Knowledge and AI Companion (Future)
Mother/Caregiver only. Two nested sections: Information & Education and AI Companion.
- Reviewed, versioned knowledge: maternal health before/during/after pregnancy, everyday food/sleep questions, breastfeeding, parenting, child education, drug and herbal reference and reviewed interactions. Topic search/filter/browse, source links; later curated YouTube links. A doctor-reviewed database is planned but not yet validated/approved.
- Companion: conversationally warm, supportive, normal chatting, encouragement and non-dismissive help with stress/tension. Do not claim psychotherapy or replace clinicians/human support. If serious distress/urgent signs arise, encourage suitable human/professional help rather than superficial positivity.
- Retrieval sequence: search reviewed internal source → answer from citation-backed content if supported → if absent and online, offer clearly labelled external search with exact source URLs and warning External information — Not MatriSync-verified → if uncertain, say information cannot be established. Avoid invented medical facts and unsupported dose/prescription recommendations.
- Medication/brand outside local database: optional external search clearly flagged unverified by MatriSync; absence of known interaction record must never mean proven safe.
- Personalization: opt-in bounded preferences only (language, tone, length, topics), reset/delete possible; no uncontrolled continual training, no updating clinical facts from conversations, no undisclosed mental-health profiling.
- Future offline on-device model target on at least a 4 GB RAM device was discussed; compatibility, latency, safety, language, model license and thermal/battery performance require testing. No actual offline AI or downloaded model in Phase 1.

10. PWA, Offline and Future Mobile Architecture
Present scope: React/TypeScript responsive PWA (or current repo framework if already implemented), Firebase Authentication, static public PWA shell with manifest/service worker, synthetically seeded Guest views. Cached shell offline does not mean real records work offline, and cached UI never makes online login work without internet. No real sync engine yet.
Future design only: platform-neutral data service/adapters; encrypted IndexedDB locally on browsers where appropriately supported; controlled record access; SQLite/native adapter for Capacitor mobile app; secure operation queue and conflict resolution; BLE Web Bluetooth adapter for supported browsers and native BLE adapter for packaged Android app; Firebase as eventual authorized synchronization backend, not a prerequisite for local critical work. Browser storage durability limitations must be tested. No forced native migration now.
Every status must be truthful: Saved on this device, Pending sync, Synced only when server acknowledged, Conflict / Action required. A locally sent NFC handoff is not automatic digital hospital acceptance. Do not display synthetic sync as real sync.

- Guest state and preferences only namespaced sessionStorage and memory (matrisync.demo.v1 is a suggested namespace). No Guest writes to Firestore, localStorage, IndexedDB, cookies, analytics, persistent service-worker data cache or other cloud endpoint.
- Preserve across same-tab navigation/reload; ordinary new tab is a separate session; don't intentionally clone state between tabs.
- Exit Guest/Log Out removes Guest keys and memory and goes to role selection; Reset Demo reseeds fixtures. Switching Guest → real account clears Guest data. Browser restore can retain prior session; never promise secure deletion on tab close.
- If sessionStorage blocked/corrupted, disclose persistence failure and allow limited in-memory navigation only.
- Guest entry should not call Firebase services. Login/signup only initializes the necessary already approved Auth behavior.
11.4 Firebase Authentication — exact continuation
- Firebase Auth modular web integration, email/password only; use established safe auth-state observer, loading/errors/sign out, session persistence on shared demo devices if already approved.
- No phone/SMS auth, SAML/OIDC, fake professional claims, Firestore profile auto-creation, Supabase. Auth UID is not clinical Patient ID.
- Do not expose tokens/secrets in repo, documentation, screenshots or console logs. Placeholder env example only; don't expose local .env contents.

12.3 UI presentation
- Mobile: 5 bottom tabs per workspace; no side-scrolling sixth tab. Desktop: sidebar mirroring exact menu and internal sections.

- Home vitals display only explicitly available fixtures; if missing, Not recorded, never auto-populate with fabricated personal measurements or pretend real monitoring.
- Don't display permanent band stock ID as Patient ID anywhere. Don't expose patient medical details in distributor screens.

13.8 Theme implementation rules
- Define semantic tokens once and use them across all roles, dialogs, navigation, forms, and empty states. Mode-specific component values take precedence over the generic brand defaults: dark primary-button text is #052E20, not white.
- Preserve exact HEX/RGBA values, including transparency. Do not approximate with nearby framework defaults or recolour the brand to match a stock template.
- Brand hover #047857 and pressed #065F46 are recorded; separate dark-mode hover/pressed colours were not supplied. Keep any dark interaction treatment documented and legible without inventing additional “confirmed” tokens.
- Status colours identify interface states, not calculated maternal risk. Never infer diagnosis, urgency, or normality from mock measurements. Pair every badge with text and an icon or shape.
- Validate contrast for each actual foreground/background combination. White on #059669 calculates to about 3.77:1: it does not satisfy a 4.5:1 normal-text target. Retain the exact palette; use sufficiently large bold primary-button labels (for example, 20 CSS px at bold weight) or an approved existing-token treatment. Do not claim universal accessibility based on the palette alone. Pale borders/focus rings may need a distinct solid outline from an existing darker token. See W3C contrast guidance for the normal/large-text distinction.
- Disabled colours apply only to truly inactive controls. Keep the explanation and Coming Soon label readable with normal text tokens; do not hide important information in low-contrast disabled text.
13.9 Layout and interaction specification
The following are implementation specifications, not recovered exact design measurements:
- Mobile-first layout; test narrow phones, tablets, and desktop. Use a sidebar on desktop and a compact bottom navigation plus a clearly labelled More menu on mobile.
- Rounded cards, restrained shadows, generous whitespace, consistent icon family, clear hierarchy, and short readable labels. Avoid decorative effects that obscure clinical information.
- Suggested spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 px. Suggested radii: 12 px controls, 16 px cards, 20 px dialogs. Keep these easy to change.
- Use a legible system font stack with appropriate Indic-script fallbacks. Body text around 16 px; primary controls at least 44×44 CSS px. Preserve zoom and avoid horizontal page overflow.
- Include keyboard navigation, visible focus, associated field labels, descriptive errors, dialog focus trapping and restoration, Escape dismissal where appropriate, reduced-motion support, and screen-reader announcements for important status changes.
- Offer Light, Dark, and System appearance. Store Guest appearance and language choices within the same temporary session.
